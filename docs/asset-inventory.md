### Verdict (revised: no cyan, no navy)

- **Cyan: removed.** It only existed on the Lucid extension icon, not the Halo product.
- **Navy: removed.** Nothing in the product assets is blue.
- **Accent = Signal Amber, #f0a830.** It comes from the hazard highlight the product's own clips use (#efa422 at the stairs). It means "attention here", which suits a hazard-sensing device. It also has research behind it: bright yellow/orange on black is among the most visible pairings for low-vision users ([Perkins](https://www.perkins.org/resource/choosing-high-contrast-color-schemes-for-low-vision/), [APH VisionAware](https://aphconnectcenter.org/visionaware/living-with-blindness-or-low-vision/redesigning-your-home-room-by-room/contrast-and-color/), [RNIB](https://www.rnib.org.uk/documents/1644/Colour_and_contrast_for_people_with_sight_loss_2020.pdf)).
- **No pure white on pure black for text.** At 21:1 it causes halation (glowing, smeared text) for people with astigmatism ([Level Access](https://www.levelaccess.com/blog/accessibility-for-people-with-astigmatism/), [colorcontrast.org](https://www.colorcontrast.org/blog/dark-mode-contrast-accessibility-guide/)). Text is a warm off-white on a warm graphite page instead.
- **The pure black lives only inside "stage" panels.** The renders need their #010101–#050505 background, so they sit in rounded panels of that exact black. They read as intentional product shots, with no seam against the page.
- **Warm neutrals rather than cool greys.** The device is brushed silver/graphite and every clip is warm sand, so a slightly warm grey ties them together. Cool grey plus neon is the default "AI dark theme".
- **No gradients, glows, glassmorphism or glowing borders.** Flat colour, hairline borders, restrained radius.
- **Fonts: no font assets exist.** Space Grotesk (headings) and Inter (body) are still fine; to be confirmed in step 3.
